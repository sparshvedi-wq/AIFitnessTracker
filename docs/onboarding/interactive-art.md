# Interactive onboarding art

Built-in image generation created four portrait variants from the existing M1 neutral render. Public assets: `m1-v3-muscle.webp`, `m1-v3-strength.webp`, `m1-v3-weight.webp`, `m1-v3-gym.webp`. Original resolution and alpha are preserved through lossless WebP packaging. Hashes and dimensions are recorded in mannequin-art.json.

Shared prompt: transparent-background premium 3D illustration for Tally mobile onboarding; identity reference is the M1 neutral mannequin. Preserve unisex ivory matte material, featureless oval head, neutral smooth chest, round capped joints and thin dark seams. Full body including feet, portrait 1024x1536, figure fills 90% height. Soft studio lighting, no ground plane, scenery, text, logos, humans or purple. Equipment is charcoal with thin Tally blue #7AA2FF trim.

- Muscle: slightly stronger athletic silhouette, realistic proportions; one dumbbell curled near chest and one held at the side.
- Strength: controlled barbell hold at mid-thigh, upright and stable; blue collars.
- Weight: purposeful natural step-up stride beside a small charcoal step with blue edge; no scale, tape measure or transformation imagery.
- Gym: sitting upright on an exercise bench, holding a dumbbell, with compact cable machine behind at left.

Runtime animation crossfades decoded images with a small translate/scale transition; it does not claim to be a rigged 3D character. Existing front and rear 3D renders support keyboard-accessible body-region selection throughout the app. Reduced-motion preferences disable animation. Question and response scenes differ.

Photo review uses actual returned evidence and uncertainty. It does not manufacture confidence scores, body-fat measurements, diagnosed weaknesses or urgency. Synthetic fixtures use the same loading and slide presentation, carry a sample label and remain excluded from real training evidence. Only the synthetic preview simulates a two-second wait; real reviews display their actual request state.
